Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 2026  ·  Effective date: April 2026

Short version: KAVEI is a personal spiritual growth tracker. Your data stays on your device. We do not sell your data. We do not read your prayers or mitzvah logs. What you do spiritually is between you and Hashem.

1. Who We Are

KAVEI ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile application developed by Leon Kaplun. The app helps Jewish users track daily mitzvot, study Torah content, and monitor spiritual growth. Contact: leon.kaplun@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

KAVEI is designed with privacy as a core principle. Here is exactly what we collect:

  • Nothing you don't give us. We do not require account registration to use the core features of the app.
  • Local data only (no account required): Your mitzvah completions, streak count, points, and custom mitzvot are stored locally on your device only.
  • If you create an account (optional): We collect your email address for account management purposes only.
  • Purchase information: If you subscribe to KAVEI Pro, Google Play processes the payment. We receive confirmation of the subscription status but never your payment card details.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (optional): We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (e.g., which features are used most) to improve the app. This data cannot be linked to you personally.

3. Torah Content

Daily learning content (Chumash, Tehillim, Tanya, Rambam) is sourced from Sefaria.org, a free, open-source Jewish library. Sefaria's privacy policy applies to any direct interactions with their API. KAVEI does not store or transmit the content you read.

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • To manage your subscription (if you subscribe to Pro)
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not sell your data to third parties — ever
  • We do not share your data with other users

5. Data Storage and Security

Your spiritual data (mitzvah completions, points, streaks) is stored locally on your device using standard Android storage. If you do not create an account, your data never leaves your device.

If cloud sync is enabled in a future version, all data will be encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing — handles subscription payments. Google Privacy Policy
  • Sefaria API — provides Torah text content. Sefaria Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (if applicable) — subscription management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

7. Children's Privacy

KAVEI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics at any time in app Settings
  • Export your data (coming in a future update)

To exercise these rights, contact us at leon.kaplun@gmail.com

9. Shabbat Mode

KAVEI automatically protects Shabbat: the app does not transmit data or make network requests from Friday sunset to Saturday night (based on your location). This is a feature, not a bug.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app grows. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email (if you have an account). Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
