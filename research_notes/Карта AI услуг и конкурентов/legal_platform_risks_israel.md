Research date: 8 November 2026. This is a practical summary, not legal advice. Items marked [CONFIRM: lawyer] or [CONFIRM: accountant] should be checked before relying on them. Where only an old or secondary source was found, this is stated. The shared web-search quota ran out near the end of the research, so a few items (patent-attorney reserved acts, the official ITA page for VAT 18%, Meta's official AI-ad disclosure page) could not be checked against primary sources. ## 1. Israeli anti-spam law (Communications Law (Telecommunications and Broadcasting), s.30A): SMS, email, WhatsApp, automated calls; consent, damages, class actions; implications for lead gen and AI outbound

### Takeaway
s.30A is an opt-in regime. Any commercial message sent by email, SMS/MMS, fax or an automatic dialing system needs the recipient's prior explicit consent (written, electronic or recorded) unless a narrow exception applies. Recipients can sue for up to NIS 1,000 per message without proving harm, class actions are routine, and officers can be personally liable. For Leon, the practical rule is: no cold outbound by SMS, WhatsApp, email or AI voice to Israeli consumers. Clients' outbound runs only on consented lists, with the label "פרסומת" (advertisement), sender details and an opt-out in every message. A message that asks for consent is itself risky.

### Cited Findings
**Scope and definitions**
- The law (Amendment 40, in force 1 December 2008) covers commercial messages sent by email, fax, SMS/MMS (text, audio or video) and automated phone messages. — [Meitar client update, Nov 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf)
- An "advertisement" is "any commercial message, the object of which is to encourage the purchase of a product or service, or to encourage spending money in any other way". Newsletters and informational messages are not expressly exempt. — [Meitar 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf)
- "Advertiser" is defined broadly. It covers anyone whose name or contact details appear in the message, anyone whose business is advertised or promoted, and anyone who "markets the message on behalf of a third party". An agency or lead-gen operator sending for clients is therefore itself an "advertiser". — [Meitar 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf)
- Courts apply a "main purpose" test. Messages that are mainly service-oriented (e.g., a policy-expiry SMS with a renewal offer, *Cohen Halala v. AIG*, 29.6.2014) can fall outside the law. Messages with links to free courses that promote the sender's products (*Glasberg v. Psagot*, 9.5.2017, class action certified), event invitations and sometimes holiday greetings have been treated as advertisements. The article says courts have become stricter over time. — [Barnea, May 2019](https://barlaw.co.il/advertising-message-or-informational-content-how-israeli-courts-interpret-the-spam-law/)
- A Supreme Court ruling held that a service text message (Bezeq appeal) is not a violation. The date was not visible in the source. — [vLex summary](https://vlex.com/vid/supreme-court-service-text-791993869)
- In a Haifa District Court class action (reported December 2019), "advertisement" was read broadly. It covered messages that promote a business relationship with the sender or with a third party whose business is promoted directly or indirectly. In that case, a conference organizer sent messages that exposed recipients to the conference sponsors. — [Pearl Cohen, Dec 2019](https://www.pearlcohen.com/unprecedented-recovery-of-damages-for-violation-of-the-israeli-spam-law/)

**Consent and exceptions**
- The general rule requires explicit, written, advance permission of the recipient, and the recipient can revoke it at any time. This is an opt-in model like the EU's, unlike US CAN-SPAM's opt-out. — [Meitar 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf)
- There are two exceptions. (a) A one-time message to a *business* asking for consent to send future messages. (b) An existing-customer exception that needs all three conditions: the recipient gave contact details while buying or negotiating and was told they would be used for marketing; the recipient was given an opportunity to refuse; and the ad is for similar products or services. — [Meitar 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf)
- A compliance-vendor blog, not a court source, says Israeli courts held that consent bundled into general terms of service is invalid (attributed to a 2022 Supreme Court ruling) and that pre-ticked boxes are not consent. No case citations are given, so these are unverified. — [Achiya Automation blog, 2026](https://achiya-automation.com/en/blog/whatsapp-marketing-spam-law-israel/)

**Mandatory content of each message**
- Email: the word "advertisement" (פרסומת) in the subject line; the advertiser's name, address, phone, fax and email; notice of the right to unsubscribe with a valid reply address (no "do-not-reply"). SMS and automated calls: "Advertisement" at the beginning of the message. Where a third-party mailer is used, both the advertised business and the mailer must be identified. — [Meitar 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf)
- The Schestowitz class action concerned messages that allegedly omitted the word "advertisement". — [Shibolet, Feb 2023](https://www.shibolet.com/en/hillel-ish-shalom-and-tal-rubinov-represented-s-schestowitz-in-the-appeal-of-what-has-become-the-biggest-spam-case-in-israel/)

**Damages and penalties**
- Statutory (exemplary) damages are up to NIS 1,000 per message, without proof of damage. Courts consider deterrence, encouraging recipients to enforce the law, and the extent of the violation, and are told not to consider the actual harm. — [Meitar 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf)
- Criminal fines as of 2008: up to NIS 202,000 for sending without consent and up to NIS 67,300 for form and content violations. Officers and marketing managers can be personally liable and must take all steps to prevent violations. — [Meitar 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf); [Bizportal 2008 headline: up to NIS 202,000](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/175885) *(2008 figures; current indexed amounts not verified)*
- The first decision (2009, Rehovot small claims) awarded NIS 1,000 per message. — [IAPP 2009](https://iapp.org/news/a/2009-05-global-privacy-dispatches-israel-first-anti-spam-act-decision/)
- Supreme Court, *Hazani v. HaNegbi*, LCA 1954/14 (4.8.2014): the court treated the NIS 1,000 statutory compensation as an effective enforcement tool and held that legal fees and costs should be awarded so that individual suits are worthwhile. Press reports say a NIS 1,000 award was raised to NIS 10,000 for 27 emails. — [Toledano case page](https://www.toledano.co.il/%D7%A8%D7%A2%D7%90-1954-14-%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%97%D7%96%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%93-%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%91%D7%99/%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%9D/); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3638500,00.html)

**Class actions**
- Haifa District Court (Dec 2019): about NIS 200 million (about US$58m) awarded to a class of about 800,000 people who received an average of 47 messages each, plus NIS 400,000 to the lead plaintiffs and NIS 2.34m in attorney fees. — [Pearl Cohen, Dec 2019](https://www.pearlcohen.com/unprecedented-recovery-of-damages-for-violation-of-the-israeli-spam-law/)
- In the Schestowitz class action, the Haifa District Court rejected a settlement and valued the claim at NIS 68m. The Supreme Court accepted the appeal, approved the settlement and reduced the damages and fees. — [Shibolet, Feb 2023](https://www.shibolet.com/en/hillel-ish-shalom-and-tal-rubinov-represented-s-schestowitz-in-the-appeal-of-what-has-become-the-biggest-spam-case-in-israel/)
- A jewelry company that sent spam to about 70,000 people was reported to pay "1,200 NIS per message". The date is not shown, and the figure is probably per plaintiff or per message aggregate. Treat as unclear. — [Ch10](https://ch10.co.il/en/?p=486270)
- Barnea notes that Israeli courts certify class actions against advertisers and their officers. — [Barnea](https://barlaw.co.il/advertising-message-or-informational-content-how-israeli-courts-interpret-the-spam-law/)

**WhatsApp**
- A 2026 vendor blog states that WhatsApp marketing messages are treated like SMS under s.30A, attributing this to a 2020 Supreme Court ruling. I could not find the case name or a primary source. The same blog recommends keeping opt-in records (date, source, exact consent text, timestamp, IP, form URL). — [Achiya Automation blog, 2026](https://achiya-automation.com/en/blog/whatsapp-marketing-spam-law-israel/) *(unverified; secondary/commercial)*
- Separately, WhatsApp's own policy requires opt-in before any business-initiated message (see Q4). — [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)

### Inferences
- **AI outbound voice calls (Israel):** an AI voice agent that dials leads with a marketing pitch is most likely an "automatic dialing system" carrying an advertisement. Treat it as fully covered: prior explicit consent, plus "פרסומת" at the start of the call. Inbound AI answering (the customer calls the business) is not "sending advertising" and is low risk under s.30A.
- **Lead gen:** consent captured on a lead form must name, or at least clearly cover, the business(es) that will contact the lead and the channels (SMS, WhatsApp, call, email). A lead sold to three businesses that each send WhatsApp messages creates three advertisers, each exposed at NIS 1,000 per message. As a message sender "on behalf of a third party", Leon is also an "advertiser".
- **Consent-request messages:** the one-time consent request exception covers businesses only, so a cold "may we send you offers?" SMS to consumers is itself risky. (I could not confirm the case law stating that a consent request is itself an advertisement. See Gaps.)
- **What Leon can safely offer:** consent-capture forms with logged evidence; double opt-in; templated "פרסומת" headers and opt-out handling; suppression lists; inbound AI chat/voice; follow-ups within an existing customer relationship that meet all three conditions of the exception. **What he should refuse:** cold SMS, WhatsApp or AI-call blasts to purchased or scraped Israeli consumer lists; "warming" messages without consent; hiding the "advertisement" label.
- Officers' personal liability means a one-person studio sending on a client's behalf can be sued personally. Professional liability insurance and contractual indemnities from clients should be considered. [CONFIRM: lawyer]

### Gaps
- The current official consolidated text of s.30A (Nevo/Knesset) was not retrieved. The Nevo URL tried returned a different law. The current criminal fine amounts (the 2008 figures were NIS 202,000 and 67,300) may have been updated by penalty indexation.
- No primary source found for the claimed 2020 Supreme Court holding that WhatsApp messages are covered, or for the claimed rule that a consent-request message is itself an advertisement. Verify on Nevo. [CONFIRM: lawyer]
- No reliable 2025–2026 statistics on the volume of spam class actions were found. The "surge" narrative comes only from vendor blogs.
- Whether s.30A applies to messages Israeli senders send to recipients abroad: the drafters reportedly said such recipients are covered ([Meitar 2008](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/83-israeli_anti-spam_law.pdf)). No case law was found.

## 2. Israeli Privacy Protection Law and Amendment 13 (in force 14 Aug 2025): databases, processors, consent, PPA powers and fines; leads, CRM, AI processing of chats, cross-border transfer to US AI APIs

### Takeaway
A13 turned the PPA into an enforcement agency with administrative fines. Fines include fixed amounts, per-data-subject amounts (NIS 2–8 per person, NIS 50–100 per person contacted) and minimum floors. A micro business (turnover up to NIS 4m) gets capped amounts (NIS 20k/50k/70k by category), and there is a 5%-of-turnover cap. Courts can award up to NIS 10,000 without proof of harm for listed violations. The first published fine for late breach reporting came in July 2026 (NIS 256,000). For Leon, the main exposure points are: (1) any list or lead business, since databases used for data-broking or direct mailing with more than 10,000 people must be registered and need a DPO and source records; (2) section 11 notices at every data-collection point (forms, chatbots); (3) data-processing agreements with clients, where Leon is a "holder"/processor; (4) contractual undertakings with foreign AI and cloud providers under the transfer regulations.

### Cited Findings
**Status and enforcement**
- A13 took effect on 14 August 2025. It gave the PPA significant administrative fine powers and allows binding advance opinions (PPA response within 60 days in most cases). The PPA signaled it would enforce using its powers "to their full extent". — [Pearl Cohen, Aug 2025](https://www.pearlcohen.com/major-amendment-to-israeli-privacy-law-set-to-take-effect/)
- PPA priorities for 2026 are proactive and complaint-driven enforcement across sectors, with a focus on large-volume processing, sensitive data, children's privacy and the workplace. A new round of cross-sector audits has started. — [Gornitzky, 28 Jan 2026](https://www.gornitzky.com/?p=30436)
- First-of-its-kind fine: on 21 July 2026 the PPA fined a health maintenance organization NIS 256,000 for failing to report a data security incident promptly. — [Pearl Cohen, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.pearlcohen.com/israel-issues-first-of-its-kind-administrative-fine-for-failure-to-report-a-data-security-incident/)
- A vendor reports an earlier PPA fine of NIS 70,000 on HOT. This is unconfirmed by any second source. — [MineOS](https://www.mineos.ai/articles/israels-amendment-13-a-new-era-for-privacy)

**Key definitions (from the non-formal English translation of A13 by EBN law firm)**
- "Consent" means "informed consent, explicitly or implicitly". — [A13 translation (EBN)](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- "Personal information" means data relating to an identified or identifiable person, including online identifiers and location data. "Processing" means any operation, including collecting, storing, viewing, transferring or providing access. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- A "database" excludes a collection of only name, address and contact details for **100,000 people or fewer** that does not reveal further personal information, provided the owner has no other collection about those people. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- "Information of special sensitivity" includes health, sexual orientation and intimacy, biometric identifiers, origin, criminal history, political and religious views, personality assessments, location/traffic data, **salary and financial activity data**, and information under statutory confidentiality. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- Two roles are defined: the "controller" (who determines purposes) and the "holder" (an external entity processing for the controller, i.e., a processor). — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)

**Obligations relevant to Leon**
- **s.8(b)–(d):** no processing except for the database's lawful purpose. No processing without the controller's authorization, so a processor must stay within the client's instructions. A controller must not process data that was "created, received, accumulated or collected in violation of" the PPL or any other data law. A good-faith exception applies where the controller did not and should not have known the source acted unlawfully. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- **s.8a registration:** a database must be registered only if (i) its main purpose is collecting personal information to transfer to others as a business or for compensation, including direct mailing services, **and** it holds data on more than 10,000 individuals, or (ii) the controller is a public body. Separately, controllers of databases with special-sensitivity data on more than 100,000 people must notify the PPA within 30 days. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx); [Presencis summary](https://cdn.presencis.com/regulations/il-ppl/penalties/)
- **s.11 notice at collection:** every request for personal information must state whether providing it is mandatory or voluntary, the purpose, the controller's name and contact details, **to whom the information will be provided and for what purposes**, and the rights of access and correction. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- **s.17B1 DPO:** a Privacy Protection Officer is mandatory for public bodies; for data brokers/direct-mail databases with more than 10,000 people; for entities whose core activity is systematic monitoring of people on a significant scale; and for entities processing special-sensitivity data on a significant scale. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx). The PPA draft guidance says any entity using a database for "extended and systematic monitoring" of users must appoint a DPO. — [Pearl Cohen, Aug 2025](https://www.pearlcohen.com/major-amendment-to-israeli-privacy-law-set-to-take-effect/)

**Administrative fines (s.23-26 "monetary sanctions"; amounts CPI-indexed each 1 January from 2026)**
- NIS 150,000 (doubled if the database holds 1,000,000+ people) for, among other things, operating an unregistered registrable database or a direct-mailing-services database not registered for that purpose. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- NIS 15,000 for refusing access, failing to correct, or failing to honor a request to delete data from a direct-mailing database or not to pass it on. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- NIS 50 × the number of people approached (NIS 100 for special-sensitivity data), with a minimum of NIS 30,000, for collecting data without a s.11 notice or for a direct-mailing approach missing the s.17F(a) disclosures. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- NIS 2 per person in the database (NIS 4 if special-sensitivity), with a minimum of NIS 20,000/40,000, for mass collection without notice, not appointing a required DPO or information security officer, or a direct-mailing database without source and transfer records (s.17E). — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- NIS 4 per person (NIS 8 if special-sensitivity), with a minimum of NIS 200,000, for processing for an unlawful purpose, processing without or beyond the controller's authorization, or ignoring a PPA cease order. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- NIS 300,000 for failing to give documents to a PPA inspector. Data Security Regulations breaches are fined per the Third and Fourth Schedules. Continuing violations add 1/100 of the fine per day, and a repeat violation within 2 years doubles it. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- **Fifth Schedule reductions:** a "micro business" has turnover up to NIS 4m in the prior year and a "small business" NIS 4–10m. Caps per category are NIS 20,000/40,000, NIS 50,000/100,000 and NIS 70,000/140,000 (micro/small). Any fine is capped at 5% of turnover on request, with an accountant's turnover certificate filed within 30 days. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)
- Commentators summarize that fines "can reach millions of shekels, with multipliers for large-scale databases or sensitive data". — [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/israel-marks-a-new-era-in-privacy-law-amendment-13-ushers-in-sweeping-reform); [Gornitzky](https://www.gornitzky.com/?p=30436)

**Civil exposure**
- **s.15a exemplary damages up to NIS 10,000 per violation without proof of harm**, for listed violations. These include not registering after a demand plus 90 days, collecting without a s.11 notice after a demand plus 30 days, refusing access, and failing to correct or delete. The court must not consider actual damage, and only one award is allowed per act. — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx); [Baker McKenzie](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/emea/israel/topics/regulators-enforcement-priorities-and-penalties)
- Disclosing personal information obtained by virtue of one's position as an employee, manager or holder of a database carries up to 5 years' imprisonment (criminal). — [A13 translation](https://ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-Israeli-Privacy-Law-non-formal-translation.docx)

**AI processing of customer chats**
- On 28 April 2025 the PPA published a draft guideline on applying the PPL to AI systems. It requires informing people in advance that they are interacting with an automated system rather than a human. On scraping, it takes a "somewhat strict" approach requiring data-subject consent and no contradiction with the site's terms of use. It was still a draft as of the 2026 guide. — [Legal 500 Israel AI guide 2026 (S. Horowitz & Co)](https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2026/08/Israel-AI.pdf)
- The same guide says the Supreme Court has ruled that a consumer interacting with a chatbot or automated service system must receive proper disclosure and transparency. The case is not named. It also reports a March 2026 Supreme Court ruling (*Municipality of Ramat Gan*) that relying blindly on AI output without human verification is negligence or an administrative flaw. — [Legal 500 2026](https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2026/08/Israel-AI.pdf)

**Cross-border transfer (e.g., to US AI APIs and cloud)**
- The Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad Regulations (2001) prohibit transfers out of Israel unless one of the alternative conditions in Reg. 2 is met. These include destination protection that is not lower than Israel's, the data subject's consent, and Reg. 2(4): the recipient contractually undertakes to comply with Israeli conditions for holding and using the data. — [Barnea](https://barlaw.co.il/practice_areas/high-tech/cyber/client_updates/transfer-of-personal-data-abroad-israeli-ppa-sharpens-requirements-for-organizations/); [Gornitzky, Apr 2026](https://www.gornitzky.com/cross-border-data-transfer-agreements-the-position-of-the-israeli-privacy-protection-authority-2/)
- PPA Position Paper of 13 April 2026: the undertaking must cover all Israeli privacy legislation, with obligations "materially similar in substance" (purpose limitation, access/correction/deletion rights, confidentiality). For security, the recipient either commits to the Data Security Regulations 2017 or declares ISO/IEC 27001 certification plus Registrar Guideline 3/2018. EEA-origin data also triggers the 2023 EEA-transfer instructions. A foreign processor such as a US cloud provider serving Israeli companies may be directly subject to the PPL. Companies should review SaaS agreements. — [Gornitzky, 26 Apr 2026](https://www.gornitzky.com/cross-border-data-transfer-agreements-the-position-of-the-israeli-privacy-protection-authority-2/)
- The EU reaffirmed Israel's adequacy in January 2024, so data can flow from the EU to Israel without additional transfer tools. Civil-society groups have contested this. — [Pearl Cohen](https://www.pearlcohen.com/eu-renews-israels-data-protection-adequacy-recognition/); [law.co.il, 15 Jan 2024](https://www.law.co.il/en/news/2024/01/15/eu-adeuqacy-renews-israel-and-10-more-countries/)

### Inferences
- **Leon as processor ("holder"):** when Leon runs a client's CRM, chatbot or WhatsApp inbox, he processes on the client's behalf. He needs a written processing agreement covering scope, security, sub-processors (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta) and deletion, and must not reuse client data, e.g., for his own training or a lead pool, or he risks s.8(c) "beyond authorization" fines of NIS 4 per person (minimum NIS 200,000). [CONFIRM: lawyer]
- **Lead resale business:** a database whose main purpose is supplying leads to others becomes registrable, and requires a DPO, once it passes 10,000 people. It then also needs s.17E source/transfer logs and s.17F disclosures in every approach (see Q3). Below 10,000 people, registration is not required, but s.11 notices, purpose limitation and s.8(d) "tainted data" rules still apply. Exclusive leads, collected for one named client under that client's notice, are far simpler than a shared or resold lead pool.
- **Chatbot and AI agent design:** state at the start of each chat that the user is talking to an AI or automated system. Give a s.11-style notice (purpose, controller, recipients including AI vendors, rights). Avoid collecting special-sensitivity data such as health or financial data unless necessary. Offer escalation to a human, which is also a WhatsApp requirement.
- **US AI APIs:** before sending Israeli personal data to US LLM APIs, rely on a Reg. 2(4) undertaking: the vendor's DPA with security commitments or ISO 27001, and a no-training or zero-retention setting where available. Alternatively, get informed consent in the privacy notice. Prefer enterprise/API tiers with DPAs over consumer apps. [CONFIRM: lawyer]
- Micro-business caps (NIS 20k–70k per category, and 5% of turnover) make fines survivable for a one-person studio. The larger risks are client indemnity claims and class actions (s.15a NIS 10,000 per person, aggregated).

## 3. Selling and sharing leads, consent records, consumer protection (misleading ads, guaranteed results, fake reviews) in Israel; US/EU rules (TCPA, GDPR basics, FTC fake-review rule)

### Takeaway
In Israel, selling or sharing leads is lawful only with transparent notice of who receives the data, an auditable consent trail, and, at scale, registration as a direct-mailing database. Marketing claims are governed by the CPL's broad ban on anything "likely to mislead", backed by Consumer Protection Authority fines and class actions. "Guaranteed results" and fake or undisclosed testimonials are high-risk. For US clients, AI voice calls are "artificial voice" under the TCPA ($500–$1,500 per call). The FTC's 2024 rule allows civil penalties per fake or AI-generated review. For EU clients, the AI Act's Article 50 transparency duties have applied since 2 August 2026, and the GDPR may require an EU representative.

### Cited Findings
**Israel – direct mailing and lead lists (PPL as amended by A13)**
- "Direct mailing services" means providing direct mailing services to others by transferring lists, labels or data by any means. ## 5. AI-generated content and video: disclosure and labelling, copyright of AI outputs, likeness and voice-cloning consent, AI music licensing; Israel's position on AI copyright and training

### Takeaway
Israel has no binding AI statute and no general AI-labelling law (deepfake bills were proposed but not enacted). Disclosure duties come from the PPA draft AI guideline, consumer protection, platform rules and, for EU audiences, AI Act Art. 50 (in force since 2 August 2026). Israeli copyright presupposes a human author, and no Israeli case has decided ownership of AI-generated images. Clients should be told that purely AI outputs may not be protectable. Training on copyrighted works is generally fair use per a non-binding MoJ opinion (December 2022). Voice and likeness cloning needs written consent. AI music should be produced only under a paid plan's commercial licence, because Suno and Udio terms have been changing since the label settlements.

### Cited Findings
- No Israeli law mandates disclosure for every use of AI. Several bills (e.g., on "deepfakes") have been proposed but not enacted. — [Legal 500 Israel AI 2026](https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2026/08/Israel-AI.pdf)
- The Israeli Copyright Act 2007 requires originality, and legislation and case law "presuppose that only a human being can be considered an author". As of mid-2026 "no cases in Israel dealt with the issue of images generated by artificial intelligence, and the question of ownership in such works." — [Legal 500 2026](https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2026/08/Israel-AI.pdf)
- Related ruling: on 31 December 2025 the Tel Aviv District Court upheld the Patent Commissioner (March 2023) that an AI system (DABUS) cannot be named as an inventor. — [Legal 500 2026](https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2026/08/Israel-AI.pdf)
- Ministry of Justice opinion "Uses of Copyrighted Materials for Machine Learning" (18 December 2022): training ML on copyrighted works is generally fair use (s.19), with incidental-use (s.22) and ephemeral-copy (s.26) defences. Exceptions are datasets consisting exclusively of a single author's works used to compete with that author. The opinion is non-binding and does not address outputs, so infringing outputs are not safe-harboured. — [Pearl Cohen](https://www.pearlcohen.com/israeli-ministry-of-justice-opines-on-fair-use-of-copyrighted-content-for-ai-ml-training/); [law.co.il](https://www.law.co.il/en/news/2022/12/22/fair-use-of-copyrighted-content-for-aiml-training/)
- Scraping: in *Shmueli v. Margalit* (55466-07-21), re-using publicly published personal data for a similar purpose did not violate privacy. In *Ma'ariv v. All You Need* (1074-05), copying non-copyrightable listings was not infringement, and a ToS anti-copying clause was not enforced. The PPA draft takes a stricter line on scraping. — [Legal 500 2026](https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2026/08/Israel-AI.pdf)
- EU AI Act Art. 50 (from 2 August 2026): providers must inform users they are interacting with AI. Deployers must disclose deepfakes and AI-generated text published to inform the public. The machine-readable marking grace period runs to 2 December 2026 for systems already on the market. — [Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force); [Houthoff](https://www.houthoff.com/insights/news/ai-act-transparency-obligations-and-the-ai-omnibus-regulation/)
- Meta requires AI disclosure for political and social-issue ads depicting real people doing or saying things they did not. — [tech.co](https://tech.co/news/meta-political-ads-ai-disclose)
- **Voice and likeness (US):** the NO FAKES Act, a proposed federal right in voice and likeness, cleared the Senate Judiciary Committee unanimously on 18 June 2026. A unanimous-consent floor request was blocked by Sen. Cruz on 30 September 2026, so it is not law. — [RIAA](https://www.riaa.com/unanimous-senate-action-advances-no-fakes-act-as-broad-consensus-pushes-landmark-voice-likeness-bill-toward-passage/); [AI Musicpreneur](https://www.aimusicpreneur.com/ai-music-industry/riaa-mitch-glazier-no-fakes-act-lame-duck-cruz-objection-2026/). Tennessee's ELVIS Act, a state law on AI voice clones of performers, is reported in force. — [Voxbooster roundup](https://voxbooster.com/blog/voice-cloning-legal-cases-2026-roundup/) *(secondary)*
- FTC fake-review rule: AI-generated testimonials presented as real customers are prohibited (see Q3). — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)
- **AI music:** Warner Music settled with Suno in November 2025. Suno is to launch licensed models trained on licensed music, with download limits, and free-tier users are limited to streaming and sharing. — [The Star/Reuters, 26 Nov 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/11/26/warner-music-group-settles-copyright-case-with-suno-for-licensed-ai-music). ## 6. Engineering services: who may sign electrical designs in Israel (Electricity Law and licence classes); what a non-engineer may provide as drafting; patent attorney monopoly

### Takeaway
Under the Electricity Law 1954 and the Electricity Regulations (Licences) 1985, preparing electrical plans ("עריכת תכניות") is licensed electrical work. Each licence class may prepare plans only up to a current limit (1×40A, 3×80A, 3×250A, 3×400A, 3×630A), and only an electrician-engineer (חשמלאי-מהנדס) may prepare any plan. An unlicensed person such as Leon may offer CAD drafting, drawing clean-up, BOM and documentation support only as a subcontractor to a licensed electrician or engineer who reviews, signs and takes responsibility. He must not market "electrical design" or "electrical plans" directly to end clients. Patent work: only registered patent attorneys (and possibly advocates) may represent applicants before the Israel Patent Office. Leon can offer technical drawings and prior-art or descriptive drafts for a patent attorney, but not patent prosecution.

### Cited Findings
- Licensing of persons doing electrical work is governed by the Electricity Law 5714-1954 and the Electricity Regulations (Licences) 5745-1985. Licences are granted on the basis of studies and experience. — [gov.il Electricians Licensure Department](https://www.gov.il/en/departments/Units/electricity-licensing-unit)
- gov.il says electrical work and the planning of electrical infrastructure must be carried out according to licence type and facility size. Technician, practical-engineer (הנדסאי) and engineer licences require a practical exam or a professional committee. — [gov.il service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/applying-for-an-electricians-license)
- The regulations define "planning" (תכנון) as preparing a technical plan for executing an installation. — [Knesset committee document](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/Committees/17_cs_bg_335687.doc)
- Licence scopes (Israel Institute for Occupational Safety and Hygiene booklet, updated July 2025, quoting the regulations; all low voltage):
  - חשמלאי-מעשי (practical electrician, reg. 11): may prepare plans, excluding foundation earthing, up to **1×40A** in residential or office buildings.
  - חשמלאי-מוסמך (certified electrician, reg. 13): all work including plans up to **3×80A** in residential, office or workshop buildings.
  - חשמלאי-ראשי (master electrician, reg. 15): plans up to **3×250A**.
  - חשמלאי-טכנאי (electrician-technician, reg. 17): plans up to **3×400A**.
  - חשמלאי-הנדסאי (electrician-practical engineer, reg. 19): plans up to **3×630A**.
  - Above each class's limit, work may be executed only per a plan approved by an authorized licence holder. High-voltage work requires plans approved by a חשמלאי-מהנדס.
  - חשמלאי-מהנדס (electrician-engineer, reg. 21) "may prepare any electrical plan and perform any electrical work".
  - Separately, the Electricity Law provides that no one may install an electrical installation or make a fundamental change to it without a written permit.
  — [OSH Institute booklet G151 (Hebrew)](https://www.osh.org.il/UploadFiles/09_2025/G151.pdf)
- A Knesset amendment streamlining the licensing of electrical engineers and electricians was noted, with updated regulations pending. Its current status is unknown. — [SEEEI document](https://www.seeei.org.il/prdFiles/2839_desc2.pdf)
- **Patents:** patent attorneys are regulated under the Patents Law 5727-1967. The Justice Minister appoints a disciplinary committee for complaints against patent attorneys (s.146). — [gov.il complaint service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/complaint_patent_attorney); [WIPO Lex – Patents Law](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/details.jsp?id=15167)
- One firm says only a registered patent agent or attorney (or advocate) may represent an applicant before the Israel Patent Office, and that patent attorneys and advocates hold exclusive rights over prosecution and preparing documents. — [Edison Patent](https://www.edison-patent.com/articles/a-brief-overview-on-the-israeli-patent-practice). Another firm says an advocate may perform the same services as a patent attorney but needs a patent attorney for technical matters. — [RCIP](https://rcip.co.il/en/service/patents/). These sources conflict.

### Inferences
- **What Leon may offer (engineering):** AutoCAD/Revit drafting from an engineer's mark-ups, as-built digitization, single-line diagram redrawing, panel schedules, cable lists, BOM/quantity takeoffs, and AI-assisted documentation, all delivered to a licensed electrician or engineer who checks and signs. ## 7. Business admin: tax invoices and allocation numbers (Israel Invoice 2024–2026), VAT on services to foreign clients, consumer refund rules for online services (CPL distance-selling cancellation)

### Takeaway
Since 1 June 2026, domestic B2B tax invoices above NIS 5,000 before VAT need an ITA allocation number. Without one, the client cannot deduct input VAT, so Israeli business clients will insist on it. An "osek patur" is outside the scheme. Services to foreign residents are generally zero-rated under VAT Law s.30(a)(5), but courts read this narrowly. It fails if the service is also rendered to an Israeli in Israel or relates to an asset in Israel. Israeli consumers who buy remotely (phone, website, WhatsApp) can generally cancel within 14 days, with a cancellation fee of the lower of 5% or NIS 100. The exact rules for services that have already started need a lawyer's check.

### Cited Findings
**Allocation numbers (Israel Invoice / חשבוניות ישראל)**
- VAT Law s.38(a1) threshold, before VAT: NIS 25,000 from 5 May 2024; NIS 20,000 from 1 January 2025; NIS 10,000 from 1 January 2026; **NIS 5,000 from 1 June 2026**. — [Herzog Fox & Neeman](https://herzoglaw.co.il/en/?p=73487); [Beancount guide, Sep 2026](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/22/israel-invoice-allocation-number-5000-nis-osek-murshe-b2b-guide); [Comarch](https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/israel-accelerates-timeline-for-mandatory-allocation-numbers-in-invoicing/)
- Input VAT deduction by the recipient depends on the invoice carrying an allocation number ("No number, no deduction"). The issuer requests the 9-digit number through the ITA online service or by API from accounting software, before the buyer receives the invoice. An osek patur issues receipts, not tax invoices, and is outside the reform. Each invoice is tested against the threshold in force on its issue date. — [Herzog](https://herzoglaw.co.il/en/?p=73487); [Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/22/israel-invoice-allocation-number-5000-nis-osek-murshe-b2b-guide)
- The ITA may refuse allocation requests it deems suspicious (from 2025). — [Sovos](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/vat/israel-new-accelerated-timeline-for-ctc-invoice-allocation-number/)
- The standard VAT rate used in 2026 guides is 18%. — [Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/22/israel-invoice-allocation-number-5000-nis-osek-murshe-b2b-guide); [Kintsugi VAT guide 2026](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/middle-east/israel.md) *(secondary; official ITA notice not retrieved)*

**VAT on services to foreign clients**
- VAT Law s.30(a)(5) and VAT Regulation 12 generally zero-rate services rendered to a foreign resident. They do not apply where the service is also rendered to an Israeli resident in Israel, or relates to an asset in Israel (Reg. 12A). Input VAT on local costs remains recoverable. Recent district-court judgments (e.g., *ISP Financial Management* 2020 on marketing services to a Swiss group company; *Hagshama Cosmic* 21657-11-19, 2022) have read the relief narrowly. — [Herzog VAT ruling note](https://herzoglaw.co.il/en/news-and-insights/vat-precedent-ruling/); [Ampeli Tax](https://www.ampeli-tax.com/post/the-entitlement-of-zero-vat); [Goldfarb tax update, Oct 2020](https://www.goldfarb.com/tax-update-october-2020/) *(from search-result summaries; full articles not opened)*

**Consumer cancellation (distance transactions)**
- Under CPL 5741-1981 (s.14C), a "distance sale" transaction can be cancelled within 14 days of the transaction or of receiving the summary document. The cancellation fee is the lower of 5% of the price or NIS 100. Airline notices state the right applies to transactions that "included a conversation" between consumer and seller, including electronic communication. Persons with disabilities, senior citizens and new immigrants have an extended right of up to 4 months. — [Emirates Israel consumer notice](https://www.emirates.com/il/english/information/consumer-law-notice/); [Etihad Israel CPL notice](https://www.etihad.com/en/legal/israel-consumer-protection-law) *(secondary; statute not retrieved)*
- A Knesset Economics Committee amendment was reported to let consumers cancel distance-sold service contracts within 14 days of purchase or of written confirmation. The article date and final enactment are unclear. — [Jerusalem Post](https://m.jpost.com/business/consumers-might-get-14-day-trial-period)

### Inferences
- **Invoicing set-up:** if Leon is an osek murshe, use ITA-integrated invoicing software (Green Invoice, iCount, etc.) that requests allocation numbers automatically for invoices to Israeli businesses of NIS 5,000 or more before VAT. Split-billing to stay under the threshold is an obvious red flag. Do not do it.
- **Foreign clients:** issue zero-rated invoices only where the client is a genuine foreign resident with no Israeli presence receiving the service, and the service does not relate to Israeli assets or Israeli end-users in Israel. Marketing aimed at Israeli consumers for a foreign client is a grey zone given the narrow case law. Get a written opinion from an accountant. [CONFIRM: accountant]
- **Consumer clients (B2C):** small businesses buying for business purposes are generally not "consumers", but sole traders and private individuals are. For any online B2C package (e.g., a "WhatsApp bot setup" sold via the website), publish cancellation terms consistent with the 14-day right and the 5%/NIS 100 fee, and send a written transaction summary. [CONFIRM: lawyer]
- **Foreign platform purchases:** Meta, Google and OpenAI invoices to an Israeli osek may trigger reverse-charge or self-assessment questions. Not researched. [CONFIRM: accountant]
