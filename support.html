Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track a mitzvah?
Tap the circle next to any mitzvah to mark it complete. Points are added to your Sefirot automatically.
What is the Tree of Life (Etz Chaim)?
Each mitzvah you complete feeds specific Sefirot (divine attributes). Tap any Sefira to see which mitzvot strengthen it.
Is my data private?
Yes. All your mitzvah data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your spiritual life. See our Privacy Policy.
Does the app work on Shabbat?
KAVEI has a Shabbat-aware mode. We respect the holiness of Shabbat — the app gently reminds you when to put it down.
What languages are supported?
English, Hebrew (עברית), and Russian (Русский). Tap the language toggle in Profile.
Can I suggest a new mitzvah or feature?
Yes! Email leon.kaplun@gmail.com with your idea. This app is built for and with the community.
