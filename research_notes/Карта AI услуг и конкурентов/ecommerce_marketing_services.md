# AI-assisted e-commerce growth and marketing services: competitors, packages, prices, demand, and positioning (Israel + global, as of November 2026)

Scope: website creation, SEO and GEO/AI-search optimization, Google Business Profile and Google Ads, Meta ads, and AI-written search content. Written for a one-person Israeli AI studio (Leon Kaplun, leonaii.com) that runs its own e-commerce store (Larso) and Amazon brand (Larsic).

Source-quality note for the writer: most price data in this market comes from agency or vendor blogs that sell the service. These are marked "vendor claim". Independent or primary data is rare. The independent or primary sources used here are Seer Interactive's CTR study, Google's Partner support page, Clutch directory data, and the Wix earnings coverage. Israeli ILS price data is thin and mostly old (2020–2022) or undated. This is flagged wherever it applies.

## 1. Who the main competitors are in each service, and what their packages include

### Takeaway
The market has layers. At the top are large full-service agencies: WebFX and NP Digital in the US, and Moburst and yellowHEAD in Israel. Their SEO starts at roughly $1,500–$3,000/month and their GEO at about $3,000/month, and they bundle proprietary AI tracking software. In the middle are boutique agencies with Clutch minimums of $1,000–$10,000. At the bottom are freelancers on Fiverr and Upwork selling $50–$500 gigs, and a fast-growing layer of DIY AI builders (Wix/Base44, Lovable, Shopify). In Israel, GEO is being sold mainly through sponsored press articles from SEO firms such as Extra Digital, SO Digital and Seolinks, none of which publish prices.

### Inferences
- The big agencies' entry prices of about $1,500–$3,000/month for SEO, $3,000/month for GEO and $3,000/month for a bundle, plus ad-spend minimums of $2,500–$5,000, effectively exclude small e-commerce stores. That leaves a gap between $50–$500 Fiverr gigs and $3,000+ agency retainers.
- In Israel, GEO is at an early, press-driven stage: sponsored "how to choose" articles, no public prices, tracking-tool-led pitches. A studio that publishes clear prices and shows real Hebrew AI-visibility reports would stand out.
- DIY AI builders like Base44 and Lovable are scaling fast, which commoditizes "build me a simple site". The defensible offer is build plus conversion, tracking, feeds and ads, not just a site.

## 3. How AI changed this market in 2025–2026 (price pressure, AI builders, AI Overviews/AI Mode, GEO). What is growing and what is commoditized

### Takeaway
AI Overviews cut organic CTR sharply through 2025. Seer's 2026 update shows a partial rebound in early 2026, and AIOs appear mostly on informational queries (about 36%) rather than transactional ones (about 5%). This hurts blog and informational SEO more than e-commerce product and category pages. GEO has become a priced product line ($1,500–$5,000/month small business, $3,000 at WebFX), and AI Mode has supported Hebrew in Israel since October 2025. Simple website builds are being commoditized by AI builders. AI referral traffic is still small (about 0.4% of retail visits), but it converts well according to some secondary data.

### Cited Findings
- Seer Interactive (Apr 24, 2026; 53 brands, 5.47M queries, 2.43B organic impressions). Organic CTR when an AIO is shown: 3.19% (Jan 2025), 1.31% (Dec 2025), 2.36% (Feb 2026). Without an AIO: 2.75%, 3.16%, 3.82%. Paid CTR with an AIO: 14.64%, 15.14%, 16.21%; without: 25.98%, 20.96%, 21.85%. Being cited in an AIO gives about 120% more organic clicks than not being cited (2.07% vs 0.94% CTR), but still about 38% below no-AIO queries (3.35%). AIOs appear on about 36% of informational queries vs about 5% of transactional ones. Seer says it cannot claim causation — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/blog/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update)
- Earlier Seer data (3,119 informational queries, Jun 2024–Sep 2025): organic CTR on AIO queries fell 61% (1.76% to 0.61%), and paid CTR fell from 19.70% to 6.34% — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-ai-overviews-reduce-organic-ctr-61-paid-traffic-68/)
- Search Engine Journal reported a 59% drop in top organic CTR in Germany due to AIOs (March 2026) — [via Pulse mirror of SEJ](https://www.pulse.bot/digital-marketing/news/google-ai-overviews-cut-germanys-top-organic-ctr-by-59-via-sejournal-mattgsouthern-769dfefa-18a6-41a0-af31-8594a0d83b15)
- Google expanded AI Mode to 35+ new languages and 40+ countries; Hebrew is a supported language and Israel a supported country (October 2025) — [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-expands-40232.html)
- Gartner's widely quoted "search volume will drop 25% by 2026" is a prediction from February 2024, not measured data. The URL slug states the prediction; the page itself returned 403 — [Gartner press release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-19-gartner-predicts-search-engine-volume-will-drop-25-percent-by-2026-due-to-ai-chatbots-and-other-virtual-agents). Israeli sponsored GEO content misstates this as "Gartner 2025 data showing a 25% fall" — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/consumerism/article-1326494)
- HubSpot's 2026 State of Marketing (1,500+ marketers). Secondary summaries say about half of consumers use AI search, and between about 30% and "nearly half" of marketers report search traffic declining due to AI answers. About 24–41% are updating their SEO for AI. The figures are inconsistent across summaries and not verified on HubSpot's page — [HubSpot report page](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/inbound-marketing-data); [GetEasy summary](https://geteasy.ai/en/reports/hubspot-state-of-marketing-2026)
- Similarweb: visits to AI platforms grew 28.6% in the US (Jan 2025 to Jan 2026), while referral traffic from them stayed flat. Generative AI is about 0.4% of retail e-commerce visits (per secondary summary of Similarweb's State of Ecommerce 2026) — [Similarweb blog](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/ai-consumer-journey)
- Conversion of AI referrals conflicts across sources. A secondary claim attributed to Similarweb puts ChatGPT-referred e-commerce traffic at about 11.4% conversion vs 5.3% for organic (unverified) — [Craftshift](https://craftshift.com/ai-referral-traffic-ecommerce-stats-2026/). A late-2024 study of about $20B in transactions found ChatGPT traffic converted worse than Google — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/why-chatgpt-traffic-converts-worse-than-google-search/)
- AI referral sources are fragmenting. ChatGPT was the top AI referrer at 722 tracked retailers in Q2 2026, down from 844, while Claude rose from 1 to 15 (vendor tracker) — [Stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/ai-referral-traffic-sources-online-retailers-q2-2026)
- AI builders are scaling, which pressures simple website pricing: Wix +15% revenue in Q2 2026, driven by Base44 — [Ynet News](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/bkqk6tylzg); Lovable at a reported $400M ARR by Feb 2026 — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/andrew-ooo/lovable-hits-400m-arr-with-146-employees-274-million-per-person-1i63)
- AI content tooling is pushing article prices toward zero: about $1.10 per article for AI tools, versus $150–$600 for managed or human SEO articles — [Sight AI](https://www.trysight.ai/blog/seo-content-generation-cost); [LeadAdvisors](https://leadadvisors.com/blog/seo-content-writing-services/amp/)
- Agencies are rebranding around AI. Moburst says AI is embedded across 29 services and launched an LLM-optimization product unit — [AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/globenewswire/3235338). WebFX launched a GEO service ($3,000+/month) and OmniSEO software — [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/seo/geo-pricing/)

### Inferences
- Growing: GEO and AI-visibility tracking (new budget line, new tools), conversion-focused e-commerce work, and paid media. Paid CTR stayed more stable than organic in Seer's data, and transactional queries see few AIOs.
- Being commoditized: generic blog articles (AI tools at about $1/article), simple brochure and landing sites (Base44/Lovable/Wix AI), and basic one-off Fiverr ad setups ($50–$150).
- For e-commerce clients specifically, product and category pages are less exposed to AIOs than informational content. "SEO for a store" can be pitched on transactional pages, structured data and product feeds, with GEO as an add-on for "best X" queries.
- Hebrew AI Mode (since Oct 2025) means Israeli brands now face the same AIO and AI Mode dynamics. Hebrew-specific GEO expertise is an open niche, given that Israeli sellers publish no prices or methods.

### Gaps

## 4. What clients complain about with existing agencies, and positioning gaps for a small AI studio

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints are bait-and-switch staffing (seniors pitch, juniors run the account), slow or poor communication, opaque or "spun" reporting, high retainers without results, pressure to buy add-ons, and unclear ownership of ad accounts and data. Freelancers draw complaints about overload, slow responses and no backup. A founder-operated studio with transparent, client-owned accounts and productized fixed scopes can address most of these directly.

## 5. What proof buyers expect (case studies, Google Partner, Meta Business Partner)

### Takeaway
The official Google Partner badge is attainable for a small studio: 70% optimization score, $10,000 ad spend per 90 days across managed accounts, and 50% of strategists certified. Premier Partner (top 3% per country) is not realistic. Meta's partner badge has no public checklist and is generally described as requiring verified Business Manager, consistent spend, Blueprint certifications and documented results, so it is harder to get. In practice, buyers lean on case studies with numbers, Clutch-style reviews and transparent reporting samples, and for GEO, before-and-after AI-visibility reports.

### Cited Findings
- Google Partner requirements, checked daily. Performance: optimization score of at least 70% on the Partners-registered manager account. Spend: $10,000 USD over 90 days across managed accounts. Certifications: at least 50% of account strategists certified (capped at 100), plus at least one certification in each product area with $500+ spend in 90 days. Premier Partner: meet all Partner requirements and rank in the top 3% of companies in the country, assessed annually on client spend growth, new-client growth, retention, product diversification and annual spend. Premier may not exist in all countries — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9702452?hl=en)
- A proposed doubling of the spend threshold to $20,000 was dropped — [Thrive Agency](https://thriveagency.com/news/googles-revamped-partner-program-requirements-and-best-practices/)
- Meta Business Partner: third-party blogs say there is no single public checklist and that requirements vary by specialty. Commonly cited criteria are a verified Business Manager, consistent managed spend, Blueprint certifications (Media Buying, Marketing Science, Creative Strategy Professional), documented client results, an annual data-use checkup, and reviews taking about 4–12 weeks. The badge is not permanent. These claims are not verified against Meta's portal — [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-business-partner-program); [Knowlify](https://knowlify.com/articles/does-it-matter-if-meta-ads-agency-is-meta-business-partner); [EngageLab](https://www.engagelab.com/blog/meta-business-partner)
- A vendor guide warns that certifications don't prove campaign results and lists vanity metrics and generic portfolios as vetting red flags — [groas.com](https://www.groas.com/post/google-ads-freelancer-upwork-fiverr-pricing-2026-vetting-guide)
- Israeli agencies show social proof through Clutch reviews (e.g., SAGE Marketing with 25 reviews at 5.0, INSIDEA with 65 reviews) and big-brand testimonials (a Microsoft manager's May 2026 Clutch review of Moburst) — [Clutch Israel](https://clutch.co/il/agencies/digital-marketing); [Clutch Moburst](https://www.clutch.co/profile/moburst)
- Israeli GEO buyer guidance: demand monthly numeric reports of appearance in ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews, plus Hebrew content capability — [Maariv (sponsored)](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/consumerism/article-1326494)
- Agencies market proprietary AI tracking software as proof of capability (WebFX OmniSEO; SO Digital's use of rocketblue) — [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/seo/geo-pricing/); [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/consumerism/article-1376362)

### Inferences
- Google Partner is a realistic near-term credential. $10,000 per 90 days is about $3,333/month of combined managed spend, so it is reachable if Leon's own Larso spend sits under the same manager account as client accounts (verify Google's rules on own-account spend). It also requires keeping optimization scores at 70% or higher and holding Skillshop certifications for Search, Shopping/PMax and similar products.
- The Meta partner badge is likely out of reach early on. Meta Blueprint certifications plus documented results are the practical substitute.
- For a new studio, the strongest proof is (a) the Larso/Larsic operator case studies with real dashboards, (b) 3–5 early client case studies with before-and-after metrics (ROAS, CPA, organic clicks, AI-visibility share), (c) Clutch or Google reviews, and (d) a sample monthly report including an AI-visibility section.

### Gaps
- Meta's official partner requirements were not retrieved (the portal is not publicly detailed).
- It is not confirmed whether spend on an operator's own advertiser account counts toward the Google Partner $10k/90-day threshold.
- No survey was found quantifying how much SMB buyers value Google Partner or Meta badges versus case studies or reviews, in Israel or globally.
